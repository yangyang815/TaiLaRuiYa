'''Moss''' is a [[grass]]-like [[plant]] that grows on [[Stone Block]]s. It comes in 11 different variants. Mossy stone requires one more hit to break than clean stone. Moss will be destroyed if the Stone Block it is on [[Biome spread|converts]] to [[Ebonstone]], [[Crimstone]], or [[Pearlstone]].

Moss sprouts fuzz that can be cut like [[wild grass plants]]. Using a [[Paint Scraper]] or [[Spectre Paint Scraper]] to harvest moss fuzz has a {{chance|1/9}} chance of dropping moss as a plantable item.{{source code ref|v=1.4.2|method=Terraria.Player.PlaceThing_PaintScrapper}} Collected moss can be placed on Stone Blocks and [[Gray Brick]]s, or used to craft [[Mossy Walls]].

There are 5 types of regular moss: Green Moss, Brown Moss, Red Moss, Blue Moss, and Purple Moss. One of each regular moss can be found throughout each third of a given map (three variants per world). They can be found either randomly on cave surfaces or in [[moss chamber]]s with a matching Mossy Wall.

Glowing mosses are special variants of moss that glow in vibrant colors. These include the Lava Moss, Krypton Moss, Xenon Moss, Argon Moss, Neon Moss, and Helium Moss. Lava Moss can be found in patches around the [[lava]] layer of the [[Caverns]]. Krypton, Xenon, Argon, and Neon Moss grow in [[glowing moss biome]]s and in much larger areas than other mosses, and only one type among these can be found in each world. Up to two ([[World size|small world]]) / three ([[World size|medium world]]) / four ([[World size|large world]]) glowing moss biomes will be generated in the Cavern layer, mostly above the lava layers, but not near the horizontal center of the world. Helium Moss does not spawn naturally and can instead be obtained by throwing any other glowing moss into [[Shimmer]].

Mosses that did not naturally spawn in a world can be obtained by putting any glowing moss in an [[Extractinator]], which produces regular mosses, or a [[Chlorophyte Extractinator]], which produces all variants of mosses. [[Moss Zombie]]s spawned in surface [[Graveyard]]s can also drop all variants of mosses.

== Notes ==
* On moss-covered stone blocks, [[Strange Plants]] are able to grow.
* Moss slowly spreads across exposed [[Stone Block]]s and [[Gray Brick]]s at any depth, once planted (including above ground). If the player does not have access to a [[Paint Scraper]] to collect moss with, they can grow extra moss by building "bridges" of stone for existing moss to grow across.
* Moss clumps will remain on [[Actuator|actuated]] blocks if not harvested.
* Glowing mosses are fully bright in the dark if dropped as an item or placed inside an [[Item Frame]] or an [[Item Flask]].

== Trivia ==
* The [[tooltip]] of the glowing mosses, <i>'A sight to dwell upon and never forget'</i>, is a quote by {{wikipedia|Morris Travers}}, an English chemist who worked with {{wikipedia|William Ramsay|Sir William Ramsay}} in the discovery of {{wikipedia|xenon}}, {{wikipedia|neon}}, and {{wikipedia|krypton}}.
* The glowing mosses' colors do not accurately match the actual colors that such gases produce in real life when used in a {{wikipedia|gas-discharge lamp}}.
** Argon can produce a pink-ish color, though the accuracy of the shade is debatable.
** Helium often has a reddish-orange glow. However, the color(s) used in-game may be a reference to how the Sun (which helium is named after through Greek ''helios'') emits a light covering the full visible light spectrum, and as such, the moss cycles through the spectrum as well.
** The proper color that krypton produces is white. Instead, the green color that Krypton Moss has may be a reference to {{wikipedia|Kryptonite}}, a fictional material found in stories featuring the character {{wikipedia|Superman}}. In these stories, Kryptonite is a crystal-like material from the home planet of Superman, Krypton, and it can weaken (former) inhabitants of that planet. It is most commonly depicted as a vibrant and light green, similar to the moss.
** Neon properly glows a reddish-orange color like helium, though other colors of gas-based lighting are often called "neon" regardless.
** While xenon can produce a light blue color, the accuracy of the shade is, once again, debatable.
* Radon and oganesson are the only two noble gases that do not have moss variants.

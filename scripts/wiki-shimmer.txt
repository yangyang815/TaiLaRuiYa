'''Shimmer''' is a shiny, lavender-colored, pearlescent [[liquid]]. It can be found at any point in the game in the [[Aether]], a [[mini-biome]] that generates once per world in the [[Underground]] or [[Cavern]] [[layers]], and always on the same half of the world as the [[Jungle]]. After the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated, it can be obtained limitlessly by using the [[Bottomless Shimmer Bucket]].

Shimmer has the unique function of transforming ("transmuting") certain dropped items and entities submerged in it:
* Most crafted items are transformed back ("decrafted") into their crafting materials.
* Certain items are transformed into other items. A few items can only be obtained via this transformation, such as [[Ambrosia]]. Some item transformations only work under certain conditions.
* The sprite of [[town NPCs]] is changed, which is purely a visual change.
* Certain enemies are transformed into other enemies.
* Most [[critters]] are transformed into [[Faeling]]s or into an item.

Items will float upwards after transformation until they leave the liquid, then linger in midair and produce vibrant lights until a player picks them up, similarly to [[souls]]. Items that cannot be transformed will follow normal [[gravity]] and sink to the bottom of the Shimmer.

In addition to its transforming effect, Shimmer also has an effect on other things that enter it:
* When a player's head is submerged in the liquid, or the bobber of a player's [[fishing pole]] touches the liquid, they will be afflicted with the [[Shimmering]] debuff. This causes them to fall slowly while phasing through all blocks until they reach an opening.
* Certain [[projectiles]] will bounce upwards upon contact with a Shimmer pool, such as a falling [[Fallen Star]] from a [[Star Cloak]] or [[Star Wrath]].
* When enemies that have no special transmutations and are not immune to the [[Shimmering]] debuff (e.g. the [[Werewolf]]) enter Shimmer, they will slowly become more transparent, eventually turning completely invisible. Then they will slowly become more and more opaque. Attacking the enemy will cause it to become opaque faster.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.NPC.UpdateNPC_BuffApplyVFX}}

Unlike other liquids, the player cannot [[fishing|fish]] or use items in Shimmer, nor can it be picked up with an [[Empty Bucket]]. Shimmer also does not have a [[Liquid bombs|liquid bomb]] variant. It can still be moved by [[pumps]], however.

Upon contact with any other liquid, Shimmer will form [[Aetherium Block]]s.

== Decrafting exceptions ==
Apart from normal decrafting, many recipes have special decrafting results and rules. They are referred to as "decrafting exceptions" on this wiki. Decrafting exceptions include the following:{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.Recipe.SetupRecipes}}
* Items which have multiple recipes are only decrafted into one specific set of ingredients.<!--Technically, these items have multiple recipes registered, with some of the recipes being marked as "notDecraftable".{{source code ref|v=1.4.5.7|field=Terraria.Recipe.notDecraftable}}-->
** For example, the [[Avenger Emblem]] can be crafted with any of the four basic emblems, but it will always decraft into a [[Warrior Emblem]].
* [[Alternative crafting ingredients]] always yield the first item of their respective group, regardless of what alternatives (and combination thereof) was used for crafting.
** For example, the [[Balloon Chair]] can be crafted with 4 of [[Silly Green Balloon]]s, [[Silly Pink Balloon]]s, or [[Silly Purple Balloon]]s ("Any Balloon"), in any combination. It will always be decrafted into 4 Silly Green Balloons, because the Silly Green Balloon is the first of the "Any Balloon" items, as listed at [[Any Balloon]].
* Certain items with both [[evil biome]] recipes are decrafted based on the current world's evil biome.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.Recipe.UpdateWhichItemsAreCrafted/Terraria.GameContent.ShimmerTransforms.GetDecraftingRecipeIndex|field=Terraria.Recipe.corruption/Terraria.Recipe.crimson}}
** For example, a [[Monster Lasagna]] is always decrafted into 8 [[Rotten Chunk]]s in a [[Corruption]] world, or 8 [[Vertebra]]e in a [[Crimson]] world.
** In a [[drunk world]], the decrafting result follows the evil biome of the current day.
* A few items are decrafted into a unique set of items.{{source code ref|v=1.4.5.7|method=Terraria.Recipe.AddCustomShimmerResult}}
** For example, the [[Copper Pickaxe]] is decrafted into [[Wood]] and [[Copper Ore]], even though Copper Ore is not part of its crafting recipe.
* Items crafted at a [[Placed Bottle]], [[Alchemy Table]], or [[Alchemy Flask]] are decrafted into a random subset of their ingredients.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.Item.GetShimmered/Terraria.Recipe.AddRecipe}}
** The randomness is achieved by making every individual item in the decrafting result have a {{chance|1/3}} chance to disappear.<ref>For example, when decrafting one [[Shine Potion]], the [[Bottled Water]], [[Daybloom]], [[Glowing Mushroom]] all have a {{chance|1/3}} chance to disappear; when decrafting 9999 Shine Potions, the decrafting result includes a stack of 9999 Dayblooms. However, every single Daybloom in the stack has a {{chance|1/3}} chance to disappear before this stack of items is actually created.</ref> So, decrafting items crafted at a Placed Bottle, Alchemy Table, or Alchemy Flask only yields {{chance|2/3}} of the ingredients on average.
* Items crafted with [[Bone]]s cannot be decrafted until [[Skeletron]] is defeated.{{source code ref|name=updateRecipeSets|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.GameContent.ShimmerTransforms.UpdateRecipeSets}}
* Items crafted with [[Lihzahrd Bricks]] cannot be decrafted until [[Golem]] is defeated.<ref name="updateRecipeSets"/>
* Certain items cannot be decrafted at all in order to prevent exploits or certain unfortunate implications.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|field=Terraria.Recipe.notDecraftable}}
** For example, decrafting the [[Blizzard in a Bottle]] (which can be found in [[Frozen Chest]]s and [[Frozen Crate|Frozen]]/[[Boreal Crate]]s) would allow obtaining [[Frost Core]]s without defeating [[Ice Golem]]s, and decrafting a [[Bunny Stew]] would mean bringing a dead, cooked [[Bunny]] back to life.

== Notes ==
* Projectiles that are reflected upon touching the Shimmer will only be reflected when they reach the Shimmer pool's upper surface. Shooting them at the sides or the bottom of the Shimmer pool (by using [[Bubble]]s) will instantly destroy the projectile.
* Statue-spawned creatures will not be transformed in Shimmer, despawning instead.
* Enemies made invisible by the Shimmer can be seen with a [[Hunter Potion]].
* Despite both being designed for use with [[water]], the [[Super Absorbant Sponge]] can also soak up Shimmer, as well the [[Liquid Sensor (Water)]] being able to detect Shimmer.
* When decrafting items whose crafting recipe produces a stack (e.g. [[Crystal Bullets]]), the same amount of said item need to be thrown into Shimmer for decrafting. Any excess items will remain untransmuted.
* [[Fallen Star]]s produced from decrafting will not disappear at daytime. However, if they failed to be picked up by the player (e.g. if they are attracted towards the player, but are not picked up by the player eventually), they will still disappear.
* While the [[Chromatic Cloak]] is equipped to prevent [[Shimmering]], being submerged in Shimmer is comparable to being submerged in [[water]]: the player's horizontal and vertical [[movement speed]] are [[Movement speed#Medium movement|slowed]] by the same amount, the Shimmer can be [[Swimming|swam]] in using accessories such as [[Flippers]], and the player will transform into [[Merfolk]] if wearing the [[Neptune's Shell]] or its upgrades. However, the player cannot [[drown]] in the Shimmer; the [[breath meter]] does not appear.
* Entering Shimmer removes and provides immunity to the same [[debuffs]] as water (such as [[On Fire!]]). 
* In [[Expert Mode]], touching Shimmer while in the [[Snow biome|Snow]] or [[Ice biome]] will inflict the player with the [[Chilled]] debuff.
* Despite the weapons' stats being swapped, shimmering an [[Ice Boomerang]] in [[Remix]] worlds will not turn it into an [[Ice Bow]] and will instead just transmute it into a [[Snowball Cannon]] like normal.
** This can be used to get a [[Hardmode]] ranged weapon very early on in [[pre-Hardmode]].

== Tips ==
{{for|tips on finding the Aether biome|The Aether#Tips}}
* The decrafting properties of Shimmer can be utilized in several ways:
** One can ''Shimmer reforge'' crafted gear for desired [[modifiers]] by repeatedly crafting and decrafting the gear.
** Extra or outclassed gear can be decrafted to get the materials back.
** Items found throughout the world can be looted, then decrafted. Some examples include [[Underground Cabin]] furniture and [[Magic Mirror]]s.
** In [[multiplayer]], excess [[Recall Potion]]s can be decrafted to potentially get [[Specular Fish]]. The Specular Fish can then be crafted into [[Wormhole Potion]]s.
*** Similarly, in singleplayer, any Wormhole Potions that may be obtained can be decrafted to potentially get Specular Fish.
*** Furthermore, most potions can be decrafted two at a time to get [[Bottle]]s, then [[Glass]], and finally [[Sand Blocks]]. Albeit slow, this is one of the ways to [[Sand Blocks#Skyblock|make Sand renewable]].
** Items can be bought from [[NPCs]], then decrafted to get resources.
*** An [[Iron Anvil]] can be bought from the [[Merchant]] for {{iteminfo|Iron Anvil|buy}} and then decrafted into five [[Iron Bar]]s.
*** A [[Furnace]] can be bought from the Merchant when spoken to in a [[Jungle]] for {{iteminfo|Furnace|buy}} and then decrafted into 20 [[Stone]], four [[Wood]], and three [[Torch]]es.
* Easy ways to [[teleport]] to a Shimmer pool include the [[Aether Pylon]] (though it requires the actual Aether biome) or [[Teleporter]]s (which can require more setup).
* Covering a Shimmer pool with [[Aetherium Platform]]s will prevent the player from accidentally falling in while still letting dropped items through.
* Relocating the Shimmer can be done in several ways:
** Using [[pumps]], craftable post-[[Skeletron]].
** Using the [[Bottomless Shimmer Bucket]], available post-[[Moon Lord]].
** The [[Super Absorbant Sponge]], [[Ultra Absorbant Sponge]], [[Dry Bomb]]s, and [[Dry Rocket]]s remove Shimmer.
* If a lot of certain [[ore]] is needed, a kind of higher tier ore can be transmuted down into what is needed. This can be helpful for getting [[Iron Ore|Iron]]/[[Lead Ore]] which is used in a lot of recipes, or obtaining the materials for the [[Drill Containment Unit]].
* Shimmer makes it far easier to obtain some items with the most complex crafting recipes by swapping their components, which means that one only needs to obtain a sufficient number of any of them instead of one of each.
** [[Fish Finder]]
** [[Angler Tackle Bag]]
** [[Ankh Shield]] components:
*** [[Reflective Shades]]
*** [[Armor Bracing]]
*** [[Medicated Bandage]]
*** [[Countercurse Mantra]]
*** [[The Plan]]
** [[Greedy Ring]]
* Similarly, Shimmer makes it easier to complete a set of [[Mining armor]] or [[Gladiator armor]], or to get a certain emblem dropped by the Wall of Flesh.

== Trivia ==
* Shimmer may be a reference to several substances or locations in media that have similar properties:
** In the 2018 science fiction horror film ''{{wikipedia|Annihilation (film)|Annihilation}}'', an amorphous phenomenon called "the Shimmer" has the power to transform and mutate living things.
** In the 1991 action-adventure game ''{{wikipedia|The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past}}'', areas called {{iwlink|zelda|Mysterious Pond|Mysterious Ponds}} allow the player to throw in items and receive a stronger item in return.
** In the science fiction television series ''{{wikipedia|Doctor Who}}'', a cloaking device called a "shimmer" allows its owner to change their appearance as well as that of others.

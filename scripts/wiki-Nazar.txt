The '''Nazar''' is a [[pre-Hardmode]], post-[[Skeletron]] [[immunity accessory]] that grants the player immunity to the [[Cursed]] [[debuff]]. It has a {{chance|1/100}} / {{expert|{{chance|199/10000}}}} chance to drop from [[Cursed Skull]]s, [[Giant Cursed Skull]]s, [[Cursed Hammer]]s, [[Crimson Axe]]s, and [[Enchanted Sword (NPC)|Enchanted Swords]].

== Tips ==
* It is much more efficient to farm for the Nazar in pre-Hardmode via Cursed Skulls, as the [[Hardmode Dungeon]] is overcrowded with other mob spawns.

== Trivia ==
* A {{wikipedia|Nazar (amulet)|nazar}} is an eye-shaped charm commonly found in the Middle East meant to ward away a superstitious curse called the {{wikipedia|Evil eye}}, believed to be cast by a malevolent, envious, or admiring gaze (depending on the culture), causing misfortune or injury (usually unintentionally) to the afflicted. The word ''{{wiktionary|nazar}}'' is derived from the Arabic {{wiktionary|نظر}} meaning ''sight'' or ''seeing''; in this case it means ''glare''.

The '''Blindfold''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[immunity accessory]] that grants the player immunity to the [[Darkness]] [[debuff]]. It has a {{chance|1/100}} / {{expert|{{chance|199/10000}}}} chance to drop from [[Corrupt Slime]]s, [[Slimeling]]s, [[Crimslime]]s, [[Dark Mummy|Dark Mummies]], and [[Blood Mummies]].

== Notes ==
* Despite inflicting Darkness, [[Black Slime]]s, [[Demon]]s and some [[Darkness#Causes|other enemies]] cannot drop a Blindfold.
* It will not protect the player from [[Blackout]] or [[Obstructed]], nor will it cancel out the positive effects of the [[Shine]] or [[Night Owl]] [[buffs]].

== Trivia ==
* The Blindfold is the only Ankh Charm material that is visible when equipped.

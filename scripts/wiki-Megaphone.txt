The '''Megaphone''' is an [[immunity accessory]] that grants the player immunity to the [[Silenced]] [[debuff]]. It has a {{chance|1/100}} / {{expert|{{chance|199/10000}}}} chance to drop from [[Pixie]]s, [[Green Jellyfish]], [[Dark Mummy|Dark Mummies]], and [[Blood Mummy|Blood Mummies]]. While these are all [[Hardmode]]-exclusive [[enemies]], the Megaphone can already be obtained in [[pre-Hardmode]] via [[Shimmer]].

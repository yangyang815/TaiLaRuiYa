The '''Bee Hive''' is a natural [[trap]] which spawns over time under [[Jungle grass]] ceilings of the [[Jungle]] and [[Underground Jungle]]. When a Bee Hive is hit, it falls to the ground and rolls, releasing 2–5 [[Bee]]s upon breaking. If it falls into any [[liquid]], it will not release any Bees upon breaking, and drop 2–5 [[Hive]] blocks instead.{{source code ref|name=kill|v=1.4.5.4|method=Terraria.Projectile.Kill}} It can be crafted and placed manually.

== Notes ==
* At least 2 Bees will be spawned. There are three independent {{chance|1/3}} chances to increase this number by 1,<ref name="kill"/> resulting in a maximum possible number of Bees of 5. The following table visualizes the number of Bees and the respective chances:
: {| class="terraria sortable"
! {{ht|#|Number of Bees}} !! Chance
|-
| align=center | 2 || {{chance|8/27}}
|-
| align=center | 3 || {{chance|12/27|reduce=no}}
|-
| align=center | 4 || {{chance|6/27|reduce=no}}
|-
| align=center | 5 || {{chance|1/27}}
|}
: On average, 3 Bees will be spawned.
* A Bee Hive will only spawn Bees when there are less than 100 [[NPC spawning|NPCs]] existing in the world (e.g. without any other [[enemies]], friendly [[NPCs]], [[critters]], etc., a maximum of 100 Bees can be spawned from the Bee Hive).<ref name="kill"/>
* Bee Hives can generate in [[water]].
* Since the Bees are spawned when the Bee Hive breaks, a long fall can put them far enough from the player to be instantly despawned.
* A Bee Hive cannot be retrieved once placed, as hitting it with a [[pickaxe]] or [[drill]], which would otherwise break the tile and drop an item, causes it to fall and break.
* The Bee Hive itself does not deal damage to the player on contact.
* Bee Hives have a chance to drop upon [[shaking]] a [[Mahogany tree]]. They will drop as projectiles that will break, not as collectible items.

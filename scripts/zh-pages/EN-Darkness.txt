'''Darkness''' is a [[Debuffs|debuff]] that decreases the player's light vision, which functions much like a reverse [[Night Owl]] [[Buffs|buff]], dimming rather than amplifying provided light.

Visually, the debuff causes the player to gain a blue tint, {{eicons|1.4.0.1}} and their eyes will appear to close for the debuff's duration.

The debuff can prevented by equipping the [[Blindfold]] or its derivatives.

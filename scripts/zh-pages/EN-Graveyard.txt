The '''Graveyard''' is a [[mini-biome]] that starts to appear when at least five{{source code ref|v=1.4.5.6|method=Terraria.SceneState.UpdateGraveyard|field=Terraria.SceneMetrics.GraveyardTileMin}} [[Tombstones]] are near the player and completes when at least seven{{source code ref|v=1.4.5.6|method=Terraria.SceneMetrics.EnoughTilesForGraveyard|field=Terraria.SceneMetrics.GraveyardTileThreshold}} Tombstones are near the player. Some special visual effects can be seen in the Graveyard, and these effects grow stronger as more Tombstones are added, capped at nine Tombstones:{{source code ref|v=1.4.5.6|method=Terraria.SceneState.UpdateGraveyard|field=Terraria.SceneMetrics.GraveyardTileMax}} colors desaturate, lightning randomly strikes in the background, and [[Ecto Mist]] accumulates on the ground.

When at least seven Tombstones are nearby, the Graveyard biome will gain additional functional effects: unique Graveyard [[enemies]] spawn here [[Day and night cycle|day and night]], as well as the usual creatures originally spawning at night. The [[spawn rate]] is not increased, but the enemy spawn reduction of [[towns]] is weakened. [[NPC]] vendors have unique trades here, and the Ecto Mist on the ground enables [[Ecto Mist#Crafting|unique crafting recipes]] for the player.

[[Sunflower]]s can prevent the forming of Graveyards: each Sunflower nearby negates the presence of one Tombstone.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.SceneMetrics.ExportTileCountsToMain}}

== Notes ==
* All Tombstones within a {{buff area}} centered on the player are considered when determining whether the area is a Graveyard biome.
** Each Sunflower within the same area makes the Graveyard biome require one additional Tombstone to form (e.g. six Tombstones are required to form the biome if one Sunflower is nearby); additionally, Sunflowers will not grant the [[Happy!]] [[buff]] if there are at least five Tombstones within this area.
* While [[night]]-only [[enemies]] can spawn at daytime through the graveyard, nocturnal [[bosses]] still cannot be spawned during the day.
* [[NPCs]] in worlds with [[Hardcore]] characters will drop Tombstones when they are killed, making it possible to build a Graveyard without sacrificing a character.
* Although most NPCs will complain when in a Graveyard, it does not actually affect their [[happiness]] at all. As a Graveyard is always in combination with a different biome, their happiness value will be based on the other present biome.
** However, the [[Painter]] will not sell the paintings [[Daylight]], [[First Encounter]], [[Good Morning]], [[Underground Reward]], [[Through the Window]], [[Place Above the Clouds]], and [[Sky Guardian]] if he is spoken to in a Graveyard.
* Normally, certain enemies with the [[Fighter AI]] cannot open [[doors]] and [[Tall Gate]]s unless during a [[Blood Moon]]. In a Graveyard, these enemies have a {{chance|1/60}} chance to open a door or Tall Gate even outside of a Blood Moon.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.NPC.AI_003_Fighters}}
* An underground Graveyard will generate Ecto Mist, but not spawn its enemies.

== Tips ==
* A large amount of [[Ecto Mist]] coats surfaces in Graveyard biomes, which can obscure items like [[Pressure Plates]].<!--This could previously be toggled on and off with the "Blood and Gore" [[Settings|setting]], but this appears to have been patched.-->
* The large number of enemies spawns, including the particularly powerful [[Ghost (enemy)|Ghosts]], can make the Graveyard fairly dangerous during the early game. Players may wish to avoid letting Tombstones accumulate around their base and [[spawn point]].
** This is especially true for [[Mediumcore]] players, as generating Tombstones over and over at the same spawn point can lead to a softlock situation, where it becomes almost impossible to retrieve items without being attacked.
* Tombstones require two adjacent tiles of free space in order to be placed. Their placement can be prevented altogether by rendering at least every other block of a surface invalid, which includes placing [[platforms]], [[Torches]], [[furniture]], or [[saplings]], [[Slope|sloping]] the blocks with a [[hammer]], deactivating them with [[Actuator]]s, or simply digging them out.
* Placing [[Sunflower]]s around common death spots such as [[arena]]s can help to prevent a Graveyard from being created without the need to constantly mine Tombstones.
** Strategically placed Sunflowers with an overlapping radius can also create a pocket of non-Graveyard within the biome. This can be especially useful in worlds where the [[secret world seed]] [[What a horrible night to have a curse]] is enabled, allowing the player to go just outside a structure during the day without spawning Graveyard enemies while inside.
* Collecting Tombstones in the early game and creating a Graveyard can be a way to easily obtain [[Abigail's Flower]] and farm [[Maggot]]s as [[bait]] from [[Maggot Zombie]]s.
* The [[Dryad]] sells [[Crimson Seeds]] in a [[The Corruption|Corrupt]] world and [[Corrupt Seeds]] in a [[The Crimson|Crimson]] world during [[Hardmode]] when housed in a Graveyard biome, facilitating the presence of both [[evil biomes]] in one world without bringing items from other worlds.

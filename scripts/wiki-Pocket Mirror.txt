The '''Pocket Mirror''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[immunity accessory]] that grants the player immunity to the [[Stoned]] [[debuff]]. It has a {{chance|1/40}} / {{expert|{{chance|1/20}}}} chance of being dropped by naturally spawned [[Medusa]]s.

== Trivia ==
* The Pocket Mirror is an allusion to the Greek myth of {{wikipedia|Perseus}} where Perseus used a shield's reflection to see {{wikipedia|Medusa}} so he could kill her without being petrified.
